Final Project

1. Create a network infrastructure design communications document that includes identified hardware components, connections to the outside world, identified physical layer connectivity, addressing, operational and security components.

2. Install, configure, and test security hardware and software tools with supporting documentation such as port scanners, intrusion detection systems, firewalls, anti-virus tools, patch management, auditing, and assessment.

3. Construct, implement, and document a script or program to automate a security-related process such as installation, administration, management, updates, auditing, analysis, and assessment.

4. Create a policy or procedure for events such as disaster recovery, business continuity, incident response, acceptable use, information security, physical security, and troubleshooting procedures.

5. Develop a research report or implementation plan concerning legal and ethical best practices and required standards related to information security.

6. Research, document, test, and evaluate current industry security threats, risks, malicious activities, encryption technologies, mitigation techniques, and methods to protect sensitive data.
